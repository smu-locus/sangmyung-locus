# A Star-Light Night Trip Course in Gangwon Province
We have prepared a special journey to experience the beautiful nights of Gangwon-do, following the sparkling starlight. From Hwacheon’s Jogyungcheol Observatory to Anbandegi and Munchijae, this route lets you relax in lush nature during the day and gaze at a sky full of stars at night. If you want to escape your daily routine and find some rest by gazing up at the sky, follow this starlit journey. The nights of Gangwon Province will be more beautiful than you expect and give you special memories. ✨

So, shall we embark on a 3-night, 4-day journey through the starlight to meet the stars?

### 4. Chou Kyoung Chol Observatory
![조경철천문대 외관](https://www.g1tv.co.kr/uploads/web_editor/2025/09/1758243057_02e9c3ee2ed1a40ebb17.jpg)

After a hearty dinner at Oksugol Nongwon, it’s time to head out to see the highlight of this trip.

The Chou Kyoung Chol Observatory is a stargazing site established to honor the achievements of Dr. Chou Kyoung Chol, a leading astronomer of South Korea. Open to visitors both day and night, it offers a fascinating way to experience astronomy and the universe.

On the first floor, there is a memorial hall showcasing the life and achievements of Dr. Chou Kyoung Chol, while the second-floor astronomy and space exhibition hall offers a variety of information about the history of the universe, the solar system, and constellations. In the planetarium, you can also preview the night sky by viewing tonight’s constellations on a circular screen. From the third floor, you can enjoy panoramic views of the Cheorwon Plain and the scenery beyond the Demilitarized Zone, and you can also tour Dr. Cho Kyung-chul’s study and observation practice room. In particular, the 1-meter primary telescope, built with domestic technology, is a signature facility of the Chou Kyoung Chol Observatory, allowing for the observation of a wide variety of celestial bodies on clear nights.

![조경철천문대 시설](https://cdn.visitkorea.or.kr/img/call?cmd=VIEW&id=4c9fda29-2bb3-48a8-95e1-73cdcea62920)

When the sun sets and darkness falls, the observatory truly shines. Under the night sky away from city lights, you can spend a special time enjoying constellation commentary and stargazing. As you gaze up at Gangwon’s clear night sky and admire the twinkling stars, you’ll naturally understand why this place is considered a top spot for stargazing.

![조경철천문대 풍경](https://cdn.visitkorea.or.kr/img/call?cmd=VIEW&id=76697b92-f1bf-4245-bb33-11f250ac9f95)

Najeon Station, which has stood through Jeongseon’s history for many years, has been newly renovated into a café and has become a popular destination for travelers.
