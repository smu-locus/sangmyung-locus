The Welcome to Dongmakgol filming location in Pyeongchang, Gangwon Province, is a set built on a fictional mountain village not even on any map. He has also appeared in films such as Welcome to Dongmakgol and The King's Warden, as well as the dramas Bon Appétit, Your Majesty and Kingdom: Season 2. It’s fun to explore the set and find scenes from the film and drama, and it’s also great for a walk while imagining the atmosphere during filming. In addition, various tourist attractions such as Yeongwol Coal Mine Cultural Village, Pyeongchang Rock Park, and Baekryong Cave are nearby, making it easy to explore them all together.
