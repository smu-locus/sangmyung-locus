Samtan Art Mine, known as the filming location for the dramas 'Descendants of the Sun' and 'Ghost', is a cultural and artistic mine created by combining “Samtan,” an abbreviation of Samcheok coal mine, with “Art” and “Mine.” In particular, 'Descendants of the Sun' is said to have been filmed at the Mint Gallery 4 (miner’s shower), Rail Bike Museum, and the Second Western Tower of the Samtan Art Center.
Samtan Art Mine was created by preserving the traces of the former Samcheok coal mine, which operated from 1964 to 2001, while adding the value of culture and the arts to the site. The first place you will encounter is the Samtan Art Center Main Building. It utilizes a building that once served as the Samcheok coal mine’s office space, a shared shower room for miners, and a driver’s cabin for the vertical shaft, housing the Residency Room, the CAM1 Museum of Contemporary Art, and the Samtan Art Mine Archive. Also, the shower room where the former miners bathed and the washing area for boots have been transformed into a mine gallery, creating a very special exhibition atmosphere.
The Rail Bike Museum is also one of Samtan Art Mine’s main spaces; it is the building housing the 53-meter-high aerial platform that is the first thing you see when entering from the outside. Here, the hoist refers to the industrial elevator that the miners used to move to the underground tunnels. The interior of the building has been preserved as it was, allowing visitors to feel the atmosphere of the old Samcheok coal mine to the fullest. In addition, the outdoor park features Restaurant 823L, converted from a former mining machinery factory, and the Museum of Primitive Art, which was once used as a central compressor room.
