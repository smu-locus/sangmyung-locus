Gangneung Imdang-dong Catholic Church was the filming location for episode 21 of the drama 'Mr. Sunshine', where Kudo Hina (Kim Min-jung) visits the Gangneung Catholic village to meet her late mother.
Imdang-dong Cathedral exemplifies the architectural style of Gangwon State in the 1950s, with its pointed bell tower, roof decorations, pedimented windows, and intricate interior moldings, all of which are highly valuable for their preservation. The domed ceilings and brightly lit stained glass windows let in sunlight, illuminating the ivory-colored interior in a beautiful way. In addition, this place allows you to study Korea’s modern cultural heritage alongside its exotic scenery, and if you avoid mass times, you can explore the interior space at your leisure.
