Tongri Tantan Park, famous as a filming location for the drama 'Descendants of the Sun', has a drama set. Medical volunteer uniforms, combat gear, combat boots, bulletproof helmets, blankets, and ankle cuffs are provided so visitors can vividly imagine scenes from the drama on set.
Here, you can also enjoy various experiences, such as live sketching inspired by the legend of Gumo and the Dragon King, an augmented reality (AR) photo zone featuring representative animals from six continents, and 'Gunsleyers,' a shooting game where you become a guardian angel saving Taebaek. At 'The Path Embracing Memory' and 'The Path in Search of Light,' which utilize abandoned mine tunnels, visitors can experience a unique interplay of digital content such as media art and music. It’s also nice to relax along the walking paths connecting the two tunnels and at the various photo spots. Especially when you step out onto the 'Path Embracing Memory,' the wide-open scenery and the refreshing Baekdudaegan wind unfold, leaving a lasting impression.
