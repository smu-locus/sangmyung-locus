Gangmun Beach is the beach featured in episode 7 of the romantic comedy drama 'She Was Pretty', and the ring bench where the lead actors sit side by side is a popular photo spot loved by many tourists.
Gangmun Beach, meaning 'the mouth of the river,' is a famous spot where soft white sand and blue East Sea blend to showcase nature’s unique beauty. More tranquil and peaceful than Gyeongpo Beach, this place is perfect for deep reflection and a calm stroll amid the sound of crashing waves and refreshing sea breeze. Also, as the sun begins to set, Gangmun Beach reveals another side of its beauty. The coastal walkway is illuminated, and as the sea gradually takes on a warm glow, the LED lights of Sotdae Bridge blend with the waves to create a magical stage of light as night deepens.
