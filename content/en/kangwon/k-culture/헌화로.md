Jeongdongjin Heonhwa-ro, famous as the final scene of the drama 'Signal', is a roughly 6-kilometer coastal road stretching from Geumjin Beach to Jeongdongjin Port. 
Heonhwa-ro is the name of a road that originated from the song “Heonhwa-ri,” which was sung by an old man offering azaleas when Sunjeonggong’s wife, Lady Su, requested azaleas from the seaside cliffs. It offers beautiful natural scenery throughout all four seasons. 
This is the road you can find by entering Simgok Port into your GPS, and you can drive along the coast from Geumjin Beach to Simgok Port. On days when the waves are especially high, they can reach the road, and above all, if you visit in spring, the path lined with blooming azaleas is even more spectacular.
