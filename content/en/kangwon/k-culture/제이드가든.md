Jade Garden is an exotic arboretum themed 'Little Europe in the Forest,' famous as a filming location for many dramas and movies.
In fact, the dramas 'Love Rain', 'That Winter, the Wind Blows', 'Angel's Last Mission : Love', and 'Young Lady and Gentleman' were filmed here.
Jade Garden features 24 themed gardens spread over approximately 163,500 square meters, preserving the natural terrain as much as possible. European vibes are felt throughout, from the Italian Tuscany-style visitor center at the entrance, to the British border garden decorated with English-style flower beds, and the Italian wedding garden featuring Italian garden elements and waterways. 
The walking paths around the arboretum are broadly divided into the Tree Scent Trail and the Forest Breeze Trail. The Tree Scent Path in the center has a wood chip surface, making it easily accessible to various themed gardens, and the Forest Wind Path is lush and cool, perfect for walking in the summer. Each course takes about 40 to 60 minutes one way, and they are connected along the way. All courses start at the ticket booth and end at the top, either in Gardner’s Cottage or Sky Garden. During your walk, you can enjoy amenities such as Cafe Honeybee Cottage and Studio Jade (a gift shop).
