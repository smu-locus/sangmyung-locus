The Sanya Cafe in Wonju, Gangwon Province, is famous as a filming location for the drama 'It's Okay to Not Be Okay'. This is the very place that appears in writer Ko Moon-young's drama as the 'cursed castle.' A set was built for filming, and the actual castle was recreated using CGI. Although the cursed castle set has been dismantled, you can still see the castle gate, signs indicating it was a filming location, and some traces from that era. 
Sanya Cafe also offers plenty to see for you to fully enjoy its beautiful nature. The 8,200-pyeong (approx. 27,200 m²) 'Complex Eco Healing Town' features a trail where you can enjoy cherry blossom viewing in spring and autumn foliage in fall. In summer, you can relax and enjoy the cool breeze at a stone table and chairs set up beneath the lush forest beside the waterfall, while in winter, the snowy scenery offers a completely different charm.
