Sangdomyeon Stone Wall Village is where the main characters Kang Tae and Moon-young spent sweet time traveling together in episode 9 of the drama 'It's Okay to Not Be Okay'. The beautiful scene where Moon-young walks along the stone wall path and Kang-tae gives her a wildflower was all filmed here.
Sangdomun Stone Wall Village is located on the road from Sokcho to Seoraksan, and as you walk along the maze-like stone wall paths, you’ll encounter various stone artworks. Works depicting the village’s tranquility, such as cats, sparrows, and snails, are hidden throughout, making it fun to discover them one by one. Also, Hakmujeong Pavilion, the pine forest rest area, the waterwheel and foot-powered mill, and Sokcho Domo Nongyo, one of the eight scenic views of SokchoThe transmission center and other historic buildings offer healing in their own right and serve as nostalgic photo spots.
