Gangneung Seongyojang is famous as a filming location for various dramas and movies, thanks to its beautiful scenery and the atmosphere of its 300-year-old traditional houses. In fact, several dramas, including 'Jeongnyeon', 'Saimdang, Memoir of Colors', and 'The Tale of Lady Ok', were filmed here.
Gangneung Seongyojang is the most well-preserved and dignified nobleman’s house in the Gangwon Province. Seongyojang, about 4 km toward Gyeongpo in Gangneung, is a representative site of upper-class Joseon-era houses and has been designated a National Folk Cultural Heritage. When Gyeongpo Lake was larger, this area was called Baedari Village because people crossed it by boat, and the name Seongyojang (Mission Station) originated from this place. Seongyojang was built in 1703 during the reign of King Yeongjo of Joseon by Lee Nae-beon, a descendant of Prince Hyoryeong, who built a house on a prime burial site he discovered while chasing a flock of weasels; that descendant still lives there today. The inner quarters, guest quarters, east and west detached halls, and family shrine are neatly preserved, surrounded by a long corridor, and outside the gate stands a pavilion called Hwallaejeong built over a pond spanning hundreds of pyeong, revealing a structure that includes a garden. In addition, Seongyojang is a valuable resource for studying residential life and daily necessities of the late Joseon period, not just its architecture.
