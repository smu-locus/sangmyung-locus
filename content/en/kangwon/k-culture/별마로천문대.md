Byeolmalo Observatory is the location featured in episode 1 of the drama 'Crash Landing on You', where the female lead, Yoon Se-ri, went paragliding. The story begins when, while paragliding, they make an emergency landing in North Korea due to sudden weather changes! 
'Byeolmalo' is a combination of the characters for star, 'maru' (peak), and 'ro' (calm), meaning 'a tranquil summit for stargazing.' Situated at the summit of Bongrae Mountain, 799.8 meters above sea level, it offers the best viewing conditions among citizen observatories, equipped with an 800mm primary telescope and multiple secondary telescopes for observing a variety of celestial bodies such as the moon, planets, and stars. At the summit of Bongrae Mountain, where the observatory is located, there is a paragliding launch site offering wide-open views of the surroundings, and the night scenery of Yeongwol-eup from the top provides a different kind of awe-inspiring experience from stargazing. In addition to the stargazing and planetarium rooms, there is a media zone where you can enjoy beautiful views of the universe, and the café offers a chance to savor fresh coffee while taking in the open scenery.
