Jumunjin Breakwater became famous as a filming location for the drama Guardian-The Lonely and Great God. The breakwater, where the characters first meet, still retains the stunning scenery from the drama. Visitors enjoy feeling like the main characters by reenacting scenes from the drama. 
Jumunjin Breakwater consists of the East and West Breakwaters; flounder fishing continues from spring to September, and in October, you can see rockfish, sand lance, and black rockfish. Also, from November to March, mullet and herring are the main species, and the best season is from October to March.
