Sannomi Ranch’s Yukship Majigi is a filming location for the drama Hospital Playlist; the place featured in episode 7 is the summit of a walking trail within the ranch that takes about 20 to 30 minutes. Although it’s not an actual camping site, this place boasts an open view that lets you take in the picturesque 600-ma-ji of Pyeongchang at a glance, making it a visit you won’t regret.
Sannomi Ranch, now in its third generation since 1983, is a black goat farm that practices eco-friendly animal welfare and offers rest and a variety of experiences in nature. They offer mountain village farm picnics, car camping, barbecue packages, wild vegetable experiences, ranch trekking, and stargazing at Pyeongchang, where you can spend a relaxing time with free-range black goats. If you hike along the forest trail of Sannomi Ranch for about 30 minutes, you’ll reach the 60th milestone. The 60 Maji, named for its smaller scale than the 600 Maji, offers a great place to rest and enjoy the beautiful scenery with its towering pine trees and benches once you reach the summit. You can also enjoy car camping or camping while trekking, and spend a special day in nature tasting black goat tea at Sannami Mountain Lodge.
