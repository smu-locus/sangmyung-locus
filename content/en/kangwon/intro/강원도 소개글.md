Gangwon Province occupies the eastern part of the central region of the Korean Peninsula, and since ancient times, it has been divided into Yeongdong to the east and Yeongseo to the west around the Taebaek Mountains, forming distinct living cultures and regional characteristics. To the east, the blue East Sea and beautiful coastline stretch out, while to the west, deep mountains, clear valleys, and wide plains come together, boasting a rich variety of natural scenery.
The Yeongdong region, stretching along the East Coast, has developed marine tourism and fisheries centered around Sokcho, Gangneung, Donghae, and Samcheok, and is regarded as a premier tourist destination where visitors can enjoy the stunning scenery of both Seoraksan and the East Coast. The Yeongseo region has developed around Chuncheon and Wonju, and is divided into northern and southern Yeongseo. In northern Yeongseo, including Chuncheon and Hongcheon, you can experience beautiful nature where lakes and mountains blend, while in southern Yeongseo, such as Wonju, Yeongwol, and Jeongseon, you can explore the history and local culture that have been shaped by the mountains and rivers.
Thanks to its pristine natural environment where mountains, sea, lakes, and valleys harmonize, Gangwon Province offers a variety of tourist attractions throughout all four seasons. Famous mountains such as Seoraksan, Odaesan, and Chiaksan, as well as nationally renowned tourist destinations like Gyeongpodae, Jeongdongjin, Daegwallyeong, and Nami Island, are located here, and in winter, a variety of winter sports and festivals can be enjoyed centered around Pyeongchang. In addition, Gangwon Province is rich in agricultural, livestock, and marine products thanks to its pristine nature, and each region preserves its own distinctive food and traditional culture. Fresh seafood from the East Coast, healthy agricultural products from the mountainous regions, and local festivals and cultural heritage further highlight Gangwon’s unique appeal.
From landscapes shaped by the sea, mountains, rivers, and lakes to regional foods, festivals, and cultural heritage. Gangwon Province is a travel destination where the beauty of nature and the unique character of its regions blend harmoniously. Explore the many regions of Gangwon, each with its own unique charm, and embark on your own special journey!
