Saembat Makguksu is one of Chuncheon's most famous makguksu restaurants, located beneath the Soyanggang Dam. The makguksu is divided into regular and pure buckwheat noodles, so you can choose according to your preference. Saembat Jeongsik consists of bossam, mung bean pancakes, and buckwheat cold noodles, making it a great choice for those who want to try a variety of dishes at once. In addition, a variety of à la carte dishes such as potato pancakes, mung bean pancakes, dumplings, and soft tofu are available. 
Nearby attractions include Soyanggang Dam, Chuncheon Legoland, Soyanggang Skywalk, Samaksan Lake Cable Car, and Kim You-jeong Station Rail Bike, all within easy reach of the store.
Try Chuncheon’s signature dish, makguksu, and enjoy a variety of tourist attractions!
