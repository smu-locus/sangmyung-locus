Cafe Neurige, located near Cheongnyeongpo in Yeongwol County, Gangwon Province, is a place where you can escape the hustle and bustle of the city and enjoy a relaxing time. The warm, calming lighting and nature-inspired interior create a cozy atmosphere, as if you were in a small cabin in the woods. Especially from the window seats, you can take in a wide, open view, and on nice days, the outdoor seats are perfect for relaxing while enjoying the surrounding nature.
The cafe’s signature drinks are 'Barley Dango,' a nutty barley drink topped with chewy dango rice cakes and homemade cream, and the 'Corn Schnauzer,' made with corn, a representative delicacy of Gangwon Province. Barley dango is appealing for its mild, nutty flavor and chewy texture, while corn schnapps offer a unique taste with the subtle sweetness of corn and the bitterness of coffee blended with smooth cream.
Slow down your pace for a moment at Cafe 'Neurige' and experience the art of slowness. A simple happiness brought by a cup of coffee and a sweet dessert awaits you!
