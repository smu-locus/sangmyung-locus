Gamjabau, located in Cheonghak-dong, Sokcho, Gangwon Province, is a traditional local restaurant that has preserved its methods and flavors for over 30 years. The signature dishes are potato ball soup and sashimi noodles; the savory potato ball soup has a great texture, and the noodles are topped with sweet and tangy seasoned flounder and squid sashimi. They also serve sashimi rice bowls with potato pancakes and platter noodles, but this is the only place that makes sashimi noodles with chewy squid sashimi and flounder sashimi.
Stop by Gamjabau to experience a traditional taste that has been passed down in Sokcho for generations!
