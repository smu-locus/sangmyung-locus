Cafe Kusil feels like being invited into a European home, with a warm and cozy atmosphere. The cafe is filled with vintage objects, dishes, and accessories personally collected by the owner, making it a delight to explore. There is also a 'YES KIDS ZONE' for families, making it a comfortable place to spend time with children. 
Inside the café, there is another special space called the Secret Garden, and at the edge of the garden stands the 'Willie House.' Six cats, including Captain William, stay here, allowing you to spend peaceful time with them and create special memories.
Spend a special day at Cafe Kusil, full of diverse charms!
