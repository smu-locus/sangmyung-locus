Hyungje Kalguksu operates with only one menu item: jang kalguksu, a representative local dish of Gangneung, Gangwon Province. The interior and exterior exude a warm, nostalgic atmosphere, showing the marks of time. However, wait times may be long, so it's best to time your visit accordingly.
The signature dish, Jang Kalguksu, is served with a rich, reddish broth topped generously with seaweed flakes, zucchini, and sesame salt. Using a sauce made by mixing glutinous gochujang, doenjang, and steamed red pepper powder, it brings out a smooth, non-greasy, and spicy flavor. The noodles, enhanced with soybean powder for a rich flavor, pair perfectly with the broth that wraps the tongue in a smooth, savory delight. Additionally, the spiciness of the kalguksu can be adjusted across five levels, from mild to very spicy, so you can enjoy it to your liking.
A spicy kick you won’t regret—fill your stomach with Jang Kalguksu during your trip to Gangneung!
