Honeybee Restaurant, located at the southwestern edge of Goseong County bordering Inje County, serves Korean dishes such as grilled dried pollack, wild vegetable bibimbap, cheonggukjang, and grilled deodeok. In particular, the cheonggukjang is homemade, boasting a deep and rich flavor, and is loved for its warm, savory taste as it simmers in a stone pot with finely chopped tofu. Located at Jinburyeong Pass, you can enjoy the mountain view in front of the restaurant, and with outdoor seating available, it’s a great spot to visit on a nice day. 
Savor a hearty meal of savory cheonggukjang and delicious dried pollack dishes while taking in the mountain scenery!
