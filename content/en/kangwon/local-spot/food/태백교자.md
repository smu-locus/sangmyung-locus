Taebaek Gyoja is a dumpling specialty restaurant where you can taste handmade handmade mandu, and they offer a variety of Korean dishes including mushroom dumpling hot pot, kalguksu, naengmyeon, and gomtang. Their handmade handmade mandu mushroom dumpling hot pot and kalguksu are especially popular, with the hot pot available for orders starting at two servings. Also, if you order the dumpling hot pot, they provide a complimentary serving of kalguksu noodles. Taebaek Gyoja offers a spacious and clean atmosphere, making it a great place for group visits, and you can enjoy a hearty meal with neat side dishes such as bean sprouts, pickled radish, seasoned chives and lettuce, and fresh kimchi.
If you're looking for a warm and hearty meal during your Taebaek trip, stop by Taebaek Gyoja!
