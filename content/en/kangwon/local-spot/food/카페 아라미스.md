Cafe Aramis is located in Bukpyeong-myeon, Jeongseon-gun, Gangwon-do, near the healing forest 'Lomijian Garden,' where you can relax while enjoying Jeongseon's beautiful scenery. It can be used as a book café where you can read and reflect in a comfortable, cozy classical atmosphere, and also as a venue for various cultural and artistic small gatherings. There is a beautiful garden outside where you can enjoy a variety of flowers that are hard to find in Jeongseon. Especially at the outdoor terrace, you can enjoy the sounds of the stream flowing into Odaecheon and the chirping of birds, immersing yourself in the natural atmosphere. 
Blended from four carefully selected high-quality beans, it offers a rich and deep coffee aroma, and the café serves freshly baked bread and cookies, brunch, sandwiches, and salads, allowing you to enjoy great taste and a sophisticated atmosphere.
Relax at Cafe Aramis, where books, coffee, and nature come together!
