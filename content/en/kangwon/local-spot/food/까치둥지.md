Kkachidungji is a restaurant specializing in spicy fish roe stew with a 20-year tradition, located across from Wonju Middle School in Wonju, Gangwon Province. A place that has earned steady love by serving just one menu for many years, boasting a deep, refreshing broth filled generously with fresh roe, gonji, seafood, and various vegetables. 
The menu is limited to al-tang, and you can add roe, goni (fish roe), or a bowl of rice to make it even more satisfying. Since it is cooked right at the table, you can enjoy the warm broth and abundant ingredients until the very last bite. Both meals and takeout are available from two servings, and takeout is offered during business hours excluding preparation time.
End your day in Wonju with a hearty bowl of warm, boiled fish roe stew!
