Located along the beautiful forest path of Cheongoksan, Ihwaewolbaekhago is a lovely café where you can appreciate the woodworking and calligraphy of the artist couple. The view from the café radiates the charm of each of the four seasons. The colorful autumn foliage especially helps you forget the fatigue of daily life, and the interior of the cafe feels warm with its serene atmosphere and sentimental decor.
If you’re visiting Pyeongchang and want a delicious coffee, be sure to stop by!
