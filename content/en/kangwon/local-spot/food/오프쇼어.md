The cafe’s name, “Offshore,” refers to the wind that blows from land toward the sea. On days when the offshore wind blows, it pushes against the surface of the waves, creating cleaner and more powerful waves. Just like the name suggests, Café Offshore embodies the desire to offer visitors a moment of refreshing breeze and peaceful relaxation.
Located right in front of Eodal Beach, Café Offshore is an ocean-view dessert café famous for its sweeping ocean views and delicious financiers. It’s a quiet, cozy spot with a great view—perfect for relaxing while gazing out at the sea. Especially on the second floor, as you enjoy coffee and sweet financiers accompanied by the sound of the waves, you can set aside your everyday worries and spend a happy, peaceful time—at least for as long as you’re here.
Come experience the peaceful moments gifted by the sea and the wind at Café Offshore!
