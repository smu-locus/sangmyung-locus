Museum SAN, located in Wolsong-ri, Jijeong-myeon, is a countryside-style museum where you can experience the resonance of culture and art within nature that changes with every moment of the four seasons. The museum follows a path from the Welcome Center and lawn parking lot to the Flower Garden, Water Garden, Main Building, Meditation Hall, Stone Garden, and James Turrell Hall. The Main Building embodies architect Tadao Ando’s philosophy of connecting the land and sky, and people, through four wing structures that link square, triangular, and circular spaces.
In addition to permanent and special exhibitions, the museum offers a variety of hands-on and educational programs. Cafeteras, located on one side of the main building, is perfect for relaxing while enjoying a 360-degree view of the mountains from its terrace, just as its name suggests. At the meditation center near Stone Garden, you can calm your body and mind by focusing on aromatic scents and the sound of singing bowls while enjoying the scenery through its curved windows in a peaceful setting.
Pause for a moment in a space where beautiful nature and art blend, and experience the ease of everyday life and peace of mind!
