The Taebaek Coal Museum is the largest specialized coal museum in East Asia, offering a comprehensive understanding of the history of Korea’s coal industry and the process of coal formation. This place was established to reflect on the achievements of the mine workers who were the backbone of Korea’s modernization, to shed new light on the historical significance of the coal industry, and to honor the workers who dedicated themselves to coal production as industrial laborers despite harsh working conditions.
The exhibition theme is 'Coal, Nature, and Humans,' allowing visitors to reflect on the impact of coal on humans and the relationship between coal and people, as well as consider what nature provides to humans and how humans engage with nature. In addition, breaking away from the typical exhibition storytelling approach, we created a dynamic atmosphere from the very beginning to capture visitors’ interest and designed a spacious viewing area to accommodate the psychology, behavior, and characteristics of the main audience—youth and younger children—so they are not restricted in their activities. Visitors can also experience it virtually through simulation systems and special effects. 
Visit the Taebaek Coal Museum, where the stories of black gems that illuminate the era are displayed, and learn everything about Taebaek’s coal industry, which played an important role in the history of national industrial development!
