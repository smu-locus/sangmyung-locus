Inje Speedium, designed based on the experience of world-renowned designer Alan Wilson, who has designed over 20 circuits globally, is an international-standard circuit that meets the FIA's track quality and safety standards. Here, you can experience a wide range of motorsports, including various international competitions, while also serving as the heart of domestic racing culture. 
Designed with Inje’s mountainous terrain in mind, the circuit stretches 3.908 km in length and 13–15 m in width, featuring a harmonious mix of 19 diverse corners and 40 m of elevation changes to deliver a wide range of driving excitement and dynamism. 
It also meets the highest domestic safety standards and is equipped with internationally certified safety facilities and ample safety zones, ensuring that not only professional racers but also the general public can drive safely. In addition, we have an emergency center linked with a university hospital and the country’s first automated vehicle location tracking system to ensure rapid response in critical situations.
For anyone dreaming of becoming a racer or seeking a unique driving experience, Inje Speedium offers an unforgettable ride!
