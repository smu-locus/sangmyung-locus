The 3rd 2026 Yeongnangho Cherry Blossom Festival is a signature local spring event held over two days, April 11 and 12, 2026, at the Yeongnangho Lawn Plaza and surrounding lake area in Sokcho, Gangwon State. This event takes place against the backdrop of Sokcho’s unique natural scenery, featuring a tunnel of blooming cherry blossoms along the Yeongnangho Lakeside Trail. Main programs included a cherry blossom walk along the 8km Yeongnangho Trail, nature yoga, and busking performances. In addition, around 40 food booths and flea markets were operated to promote the local content business through the participation of local small business owners. At night, a cherry blossom light tunnel is created to offer a beautiful view both day and night, with the festival’s goal being to provide visitors with a perfect spring retreat in their daily lives.

※ Event Details: Main Programs (Yeongnangho Cherry Blossom Tunnel & Firefly Starlight Zone, Cherry Blossom Busking & Stage), Side Programs (Visible Radio 'Yeongnangho On Air'), Consumer Participation Programs (Yeongnangho Local Flea Market), Others (Cherry Blossom Picnic F&B Zone, Emotional Relaxation Zone)
