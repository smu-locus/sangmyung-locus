KOICA World Friends Village is a multi-cultural experience space where you can see, hear, and enjoy the history and cultures of various countries around the world firsthand. The exhibition experience center is divided into five regional halls: Central Asia, Southwest Asia, Northeast Asia, Southeast Asia, and Africa. Each building was designed to reflect the characteristics of the local architectural style, and every exhibition and experience center features a character named after a friend from the language of the country it represents. At the five regional exhibition centers, you can experience the traditional cultures and lifestyles of various countries such as Uzbekistan, Nepal, Mongolia, Myanmar, and Uganda. Through a variety of sensory experiences such as trying on traditional clothing and playing musical instruments, it offers a special feeling of traveling the world.
At the KOICA Development Cooperation Office, you can broaden your perspective as a global citizen by learning about Korea’s history in international development cooperation and the various issues the world needs to address. Both the exhibition and educational programs are free, and from guided tours with professional docents to age-appropriate global citizenship education, they are open to children, teenagers, and adults alike.You can take a leisurely walk on the spacious outdoor lawn or create memories with nature as your backdrop, and the on-site restaurant and café make it an ideal spot for family outings or group field trips. 
Embark on a special journey around the world at KOICA World Friends Village, where you can experience the cultures and lives of many countries firsthand and learn about the meaning of international development cooperation!
