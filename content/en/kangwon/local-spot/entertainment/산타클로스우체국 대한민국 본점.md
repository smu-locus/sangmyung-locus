The Hwacheon Santa Claus Post Office Korea Headquarters is the Korean branch of the Santa Post Office in Rovaniemi, Finland, the hometown of Santa Claus. Here, you can send letters to Santa Claus any day of the year. Also, if you write a letter with all your wishes and submit it by the end of October, you can receive a reply from Santa Claus in Finland during the Christmas season, creating special memories for your children. In addition, you can dress up as Santa Claus for photos, enjoy various small events, and participate in hands-on programs like making Christmas cookies and crafts. Souvenirs such as dolls and postcards from the Santa post office in Finland are also available for purchase. 
How about sending a letter to Santa Claus and taking commemorative photos in his costume to briefly relive our childhood wonder?
