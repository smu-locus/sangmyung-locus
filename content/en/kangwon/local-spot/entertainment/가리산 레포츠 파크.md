Garisan Leisure Sports Park is the largest comprehensive leisure sports theme park in Korea, located within the Hongcheon Garisan Natural Recreation Forest. You can blow away everyday stress with a variety of leisure sports programs, including flying ziplines, forest adventures, GPR survival, and drone experiences.
Flying Zip runs along the Garisan mountain range, with 17 lines spaced 150 meters apart over a distance of about 1 km. Enjoy exhilarating aerial gliding while taking in the beautiful scenery that changes with each season. Forest Adventure is an aerial obstacle course designed with natural wood and ropes, offering fun while also fostering courage, a spirit of challenge, and perseverance, making it enjoyable for people of all ages and genders. Finally, the GPR survival experience introduces a BB gun that provides a realistic impact and a GPR system equipped with wireless network capabilities. Artificial structures designed to look like real battlefields are set against a lush forest backdrop, making the survival experience even more immersive. 
At Garisan Leisure Sports Park, a place where people of all ages can experience the harmony between nature and people and gain confidence and a sense of accomplishment, start a special adventure today!
