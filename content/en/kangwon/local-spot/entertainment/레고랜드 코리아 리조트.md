LEGOLAND® Korea Resort is the first global theme park in Korea and the 10th LEGOLAND resort in the world, located in Chuncheon, Gangwon Province. Opened on Children’s Day, May 5, 2022, it features new rides and safety facilities, and offers a variety of shows and events so families can create wonderful memories together—during the day at LEGOLAND Park and in the evening at the LEGOLAND Hotel. It primarily serves children aged 2 to 12 and their families, featuring approximately 40 rides, entertainment shows, and 154 LEGOLAND Hotel rooms decorated in four LEGO themes. 
Create unforgettable memories with your whole family while enjoying a variety of rides and activities in this exciting world filled with LEGO bricks!
