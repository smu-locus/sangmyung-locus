The Hwacheon Ice Country Mountain Trout Festival, held in Hwacheon, Gangwon Special Self-Governing Province, is a unique winter festival that was named one of the “7 Wonders of Winter” by CNN in 2011. Held every January when the Hwacheon Stream—famous for its crystal-clear waters—freezes solid, the festival offers experiences such as ice fishing and catching mountain trout with bare hands—a fish often called the “Queen of the Valley.” In addition to the thrill of ice fishing for mountain trout—which you can enjoy fresh as sashimi or grilled right away—the festival features a variety of winter activities such as ice sledding, snow sledding, and bobsledding, attracting over 1 million visitors annually.

※ Event Details: Mountain Trout Experiences (Ice Fishing (on-site/reservation), Bare-Handed Catching, Lure Fishing), Snow/Ice Activities (Snow Sledding, Ice Sledding, Sky-Splitting, Ice Castle Slide, Ice Soccer, Curling, Figure Skating, Ice Bubble Suit, Seated Badminton), Culture/Events (Festival Opening Plaza, Hwacheon Luck-of-the-Draw Event, Ice Kingdom Broadcasting Station, Hoguk-i Experience Zone, Hwacheon Tourism Promotion Center, etc.), Convenience/Safety (General Information Center, Fishing Guides, Warm-up/Toddler Play Area, Mobility Assistants, Medical Center, Disaster Rescue Team, Hwacheon Fire Station, etc.), Food/Shopping (Mountain Trout Restaurants, Mountain Trout Sashimi/Grill Centers, Local Snack Stalls, Agricultural Specialty Product Shops, Souvenir Shops, etc.), Related Events and Tourist Attractions (Lantern Street Festival, World’s Largest Indoor Ice Sculpture Plaza, Hwacheon Mountain Trout Park Golf Course, Santa Post Office Korea Headquarters, Baekam Mountain Cable Car, etc.)
