Rail bikes now run on the old Gyeongchun Line tracks, which served their purpose as a double-track electrified line, instead of trains. Gangchon Rail Park departs right next to Kim Yujeong Station on the Gyeongchun Line. At the boarding area, there are plenty of small attractions, including photo zones featuring large book sculptures and colorful umbrella displays that highlight local culture, as well as a café and a zip wire. 
Starting from Kim Yujeong Station, the entire 8.5 km course goes to the old Gangchon Station, with the first 6 km taken by rail bike and the remaining 2.5 km by romantic train. The rail bike section is mostly downhill, making it easy to ride, and features idyllic scenery of mountains, fields, and rivers, along with tunnels decorated with various themes. There is also a VR (virtual reality) experience zone, which you can use for an additional fee. This exotic, romantic train runs along the Bukhan River, offering expansive views. After you arrive at your destination, you can take a shuttle bus back to the boarding area. 
Enjoy the beautiful scenery along the old railway at Gangchon Rail Park!
