Haslla Art World is a comprehensive art space where nature, people, and art coexist in harmony. The 33,000-pyeong sculpture park was handcrafted using the natural slopes and mountainous terrain to minimize environmental damage, following the themes of 'Seongseong Wide-Lot Path,' 'Pine Tree Garden,' 'Plaza of Time,' 'Sea Garden,' 'Sky Observatory,' and 'Stone Gallery and Cow Manure Museum,' with paths designed to offer the most beautiful views of nature and transformed into an artistic garden. 
'Haslla' is the old name for Gangneung during the Goguryeo period. Here, year-round art projects, performances, exhibitions, and festivals featuring both domestic and international artists offer a unique experience and happiness. Inside, there is the 'Museum of Contemporary Art,' featuring over 200 contemporary works, and the 'Pinocchio & Marionette Museum,' displaying marionette and Pinocchio pieces collected from various European countries. At the museum, special exhibitions, invitational exhibitions, and permanent exhibitions are available depending on the schedule. 
How about creating a day that will stay in your heart by slowly strolling through a garden where nature and art come together?
