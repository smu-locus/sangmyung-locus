Arte Museum Gangneung is the third permanent immersive media art exhibition space District is presenting near Gyeongpo Lake in Gangneung in December 2021. The exhibition hall consists of 11 themed exhibition rooms combining different natural materials and distinctive spaces, along with an experiential F&B facility and a souvenir shop. Each exhibition hall presents works under the theme of 'ETERNAL NATURE' using cutting-edge media art technology, offering an immersive experience beyond the limits of digital technology with diverse visual effects, grand and beautiful music, and scents. Especially in the main exhibition hall, the 'Garden Hall,' which spans about 200 pyeong, visitors can experience a deeply moving display themed "Gangwon, Beauty Crafted by Nature," where the stunning natural scenery of Gangwon Province is enhanced by the beautiful melodies of the traditional Korean musician Song So-hee. Additionally, the food and beverage pavilion (TEABAR) and interactive F&B facility (TEA BAR) offer an engaging experience where visitors can enjoy live garnishes presented through media art and savor their sweet flavors and aromas with all their senses. 
Experience the beauty of nature through immersive media art blending light, sound, and scent at Arte Museum Gangneung!
