Opened in 2022, Anheung Steamed Bun Morakmorak Village is a multi-cultural space filled with sights and activities centered on Hoengseong’s signature specialty, Anheung steamed buns. Filled with attractions such as the history of Anheung steamed buns, steamed bun making, dessert making, and VR experiences, it’s a place where people of all ages can create wonderful memories. This place consists of 'MorakMorak Steamed Bun Hall,' where you can experience making Anheung steamed buns; 'MorakMorak Lounge,' which promotes Anheung steamed buns and features a ticket booth, information center, media art, and a VR experience space for all five senses; 'MorakMorak Workshop,' where you can enjoy activities like making your own cup and red bean compresses; and a health playground, MorakMorak Plaza, and a pond. 
On a steaming, warm day like a steamed bun, create joyful memories in Anheung Jjinppang Morak Morak Village!
