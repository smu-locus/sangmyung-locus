Every June, the Murung Byeol Yucheonji, transformed from an abandoned quarry of gray limestone into a multi-cultural space embracing an emerald lake and fragrant gardens, hosts a Lavender Festival. Set against the unique and beautiful scenery of Mureung Byeol Yucheonji, visitors can stroll through the expansive lavender gardens and take in the stunning natural views. Especially at night, the added lighting creates a unique atmosphere different from the daytime. In addition, a variety of performances and hands-on programs will be held throughout the day and night to enrich the festival, and you can also find special lavender-themed foods, related merchandise, and Donghae City goods!

※ Event Details: Main programs (Lavender Class, Purple Color Studio (Personal Color), AI One Shot, Singing Bowl Meditation Experience, Dream Balloon Show, Acoustic Lavender Busking, etc.); Side programs (Lavender Pop-up Store, Lavender Specialty Sales Booth, Mureung Byeol Yucheonji Star Run Challenge, Night Lure and Lighting Show, etc.); Consumer participation programs (Dress Code Event, SNS Hashtag Event, etc.); Others (Lavender Photo Spot Accessory Rental, Donghae City Tourism Goods Sales Booth, Food Booth, Activities, Water Leisure, etc.)
