Kim Byeong-yeon, known as Nango, a wandering poet of the late Joseon period who wore a traditional conical hat and satirized authority and hypocrisy, left this world. Every autumn in Yeongwol, the Kim Satgat Cultural Festival brings together the spirit of free poetry, humor, and the value of refined leisure he left behind, viewed through the lens of contemporary poetry.
Since its inception in 1998, the Gimsatgat Cultural Festival has been Yeongwol’s premier literary and cultural event honoring the life and literary value of Gimsatgat and carrying on his spirit with today’s generation. Main events include the Kim Satgat Memorial Rite honoring the artistic spirit of Kim Satgat and the Kim Satgat Literary Award, a Joseon-era civil service examination stage reenacting the traditional local exams, a walk along the Kim Satgat Humor Trail following the footsteps of poets while wearing a satgat and dopo, as well as programs that explore the depth of literature such as a national calligraphy contest, a writing competition, meetings with writers, and symposiums. With performances, exhibitions, stages for local artists, and interactive content, it transforms literature—which may have seemed difficult—into a festival where you can see, hear, walk, and enjoy it firsthand. 
Kim Sat-gat, who captured the world in a single poem and twisted the era with a single laugh. Follow the path of satire and humor he left behind and discover a new joy of literature in Yeongwol!
