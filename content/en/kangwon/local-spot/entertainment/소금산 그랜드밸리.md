Since the opening of the Sogeumsan Suspension Bridge, Sogeumsan Grand Valley has added various tourist facilities, making it a complex cultural destination where visitors can enjoy thrilling experiences alongside the stunning scenery of Sogeumsan and Ganhyeonsan. Here, you can explore various attractions such as the Sogeumsan Suspension Bridge and Deck Trail, Sogeum Jando, the Sky Tower, and the Sogeumsan Woorung Bridge.
The Sogeumsan Suspension Bridge is 200 meters long, 100 meters high, and 1.5 meters wide, making it a great place for both thrills and relaxation, and the deck trail connecting to the bridge is perfect for walking while enjoying phytoncides. The Salt Cliff, built into a 200-meter-high cliff, stretches 360 meters and offers a thrilling experience, while Sky Tower Zone, located 150 meters above ground along the steep cliff, provides a panoramic view of Sogeumsan’s dramatic rock formations and beautiful nature. Additionally, from May to October, the 'Naora Show' takes place at night, offering a chance to enjoy beautiful music and create special, romantic memories, providing a different kind of romance from the daytime. In addition, you can enjoy the scenery of Sogeumsan from a cable car, which can accommodate up to 4,000 people per day, but please note that it may close early if there is a large waiting list. 
Enjoy an unforgettable trip to Sogeumsan Grand Valley, where thrilling excitement, stunning nature, and spectacular shows await you!
