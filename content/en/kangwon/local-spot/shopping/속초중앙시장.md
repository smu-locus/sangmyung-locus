Sokcho Central Market is filled with fresh seafood and local specialties from the East Coast, offering a variety of foods such as stuffed squid, North Korean sundae, and fried chicken. A large commercial building with one basement and two above-ground floors blends with specialized alley markets, offering a rich variety of sights to see. 
On the first floor of the commercial building, you can find fabric, vegetables, and food stores, while the second floor houses a clothing and household goods store, a general merchandise shop, and a Nonghyup Hanaro Mart, offering a wide variety of products. There is also a sashimi center in the basement where you can enjoy fresh seafood and live fish at affordable prices. 
The alley market is divided into five sections based on product categories, such as the fresh produce alley, the sundae (blood sausage) alley, and the salted seafood market alley. The fresh produce, fish, and dried seafood laid out on the tables keep visitors coming back, and the bustling Dakjeon Alley is filled with many crispy, spicy fried chicken shops, each winning over tourists’ taste buds with its unique expertise. All the alley markets have covered walkways, so you can visit comfortably even in the rain, and they offer a clean, pleasant environment and ample parking, making them easy to visit. 
At Sokcho Central Market, where you can enjoy fresh seafood from the East Coast and the unique market atmosphere of Sokcho, savor delicious food and feel the warm, lively market spirit!
