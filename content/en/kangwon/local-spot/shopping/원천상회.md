Located in Hanam-myeon, Hwacheon-gun, Gangwon Province, Woncheon Sanghoe is a humble rural supermarket that has everything except what it doesn’t, and it was also a filming location for the drama 'Accidental Boss.' The name 'pharmacy, ticket booth, general goods, alcohol, groceries, rest area' is posted on the windows; it serves as a playground and rest stop for the village elders, and at times even as a restaurant.

※ Recommended route: Woncheon Sanghoe → Arles Theme Arboretum → Paroho Observatory
