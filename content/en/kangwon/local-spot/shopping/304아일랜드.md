304 Island is a design stationery store in Chuncheon that opened in 2022, conveniently located across from Namchuncheon Station, making it easily accessible for both visitors and locals. They create and sell items for record-keepers, and you can also find local brand products. They continuously create seasonal items, making it a great place to visit even when giving gifts. They also sell books curated by the operator, with magnets and diaries being the main items. We also often hold meetups and classes in our offline space.
If you want to capture everyday moments more meaningfully, stop by 304 Island!
