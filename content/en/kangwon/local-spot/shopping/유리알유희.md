Yurial Yoohee is a small goods store near Gangmun Beach where you can find glass artworks and souvenirs inspired by the colors and scenery of the Gangneung sea. The store displays items from various artists and brands, including sun catchers that diffuse sunlight, glassware, vases, accessories, bookmarks, postcards, candles, and fabric products. They also showcase products made from sea-grown sea glass and East Coast sand, making it fun to compare the different colors and textures of each material. After a walk overlooking Gangmun Beach, it’s a great place to pick out souvenirs or everyday items, and it’s also nice to visit nearby Gyeongpo Beach, Gyeongpo Lake, and the Heo Gyun and Heo Nanseolheon Memorial Park. They also offer one-day classes at the nearby 'Yuyujajeok' café. 
Among glass pieces reflecting the light of the sea, find your own little piece to cherish your travel memories for a long time!
