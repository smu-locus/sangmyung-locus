Located in the Goseokjeong tourist area, Today's Farmer is a leading local food direct market that offers consumers farm products lovingly cultivated by Cheorwon’s farmers, delivering both freshness and trust. Inside the spacious and comfortable store, you’ll find a neat display of a variety of processed foods, including seasonal produce such as paprika and mushrooms, as well as rice cakes, handmade tofu, Odae rice castella, and fermented foods, featuring Cheorwon Odae rice, one of Korea’s premium rice varieties. Each product clearly displays the farm’s name and production information, so visitors can purchase with confidence and trust. After enjoying the stunning natural scenery of Goseokjeong and the Hantan River area, it’s a great idea to visit the store, take your time browsing specialty products made with care by local farmers, and add some clean, wholesome Korean food to your shopping basket.
Capture a day in Cheorwon with Today's Farmer and remember it through the fresh flavors of the region!
