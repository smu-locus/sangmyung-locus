Gorae Bookstore is an independent bookstore and bakery book café located in Gangneung, and its name combines 'GO,' meaning “to go,” and “re,” meaning “again,” to convey the idea of “let’s go again.” This is not just a place that sells books, but a cultural space that captures the city of Gangneung and breathes with its local community. 
Whale Bookstore aims to be a space where you can discover books by writers from Gangneung at any time, introduce local artists active in the area, and listen to experts from various fields to foster coexistence and development. From the basement to the fourth floor, a variety of spaces including a standalone bookstore, gift shop, bakery, café, children’s bookstore, and seminar rooms are thoughtfully arranged, allowing you to enjoy both books and culture. 
At Whale Bookstore, where the scent of books blends with the aroma of coffee, experience the culture and sensibility of Gangneung and add a touch of inspiration and relaxation to your daily life!
