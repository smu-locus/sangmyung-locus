Yemil Wine Healing Center is a winery operated by the agricultural cooperative in Yemil 2-ri, Gimsatgat-myeon, Yeongwol. In Yemil Village, where ideal natural conditions for grape cultivation are met, the Campbell Early variety of grapes, known for its high polyphenol content and sweetness, is grown using eco-friendly farming methods, and the village is famous for its own brand of wine made from these grapes. In addition to tasting and purchasing Yemil wine, you can also try making wine and enjoy a foot bath.
Create unforgettable memories at the Yemil Wine Healing Center, where the aroma and taste of wine blend into a vibrant experience!
