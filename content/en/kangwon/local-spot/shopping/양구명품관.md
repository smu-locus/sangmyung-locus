Yanggu Premium Hall showcases high-quality agricultural specialties and tourism products grown in Yanggu. They have created their own brand, 'Nature Center,' reflecting Yanggu County’s clean and pristine image and geographic features as the heart of eco-friendly natural farming in the center of the country, reducing middlemen and selling their products to consumers at affordable prices. In the pristine region of Yanggu, you can find rice, grains, honey, flowerpots, melons, paprika, dried radish greens, shiitake mushrooms, and pumpkin—all grown and harvested locally—as well as a wide variety of fresh foods and processed products like yakgwa and pickled vegetables. 
If you visit Yanggu, why not pick out a personalized gift at the Yanggu Premium Hall, one of the must-visit places?
