Mukho Port in Donghae, Gangwon Province, opened in 1937 as the first trading port on the East Coast and has since become a key fishing base there, most famously known for its live fish sales center. If you time your visit to Mukho Port to coincide with the early morning arrival of fishing boats, you can find freshly caught sashimi at the fish market, and watching the auction of the catch is a unique experience. There are also shops at the port where you can shop for dried seafood and more.
Mukho Lighthouse, a manned lighthouse built in June 1963, stands on Dongmunsan in Mukho Port. It is a 12-meter-tall, two-story circular structure made of reinforced concrete, and its pure white design blends beautifully with the blue sea, creating a clear and pristine image. A park surrounds the lighthouse with panoramic views of the sea, and as soon as you enter the small park, a sculpture inscribed with the words 'From the Sun to the Boy' stretches out. In addition, there are many attractions nearby such as Dojaebi-gol Sky Valley, Nongoldam-gil, Mukho Port Waterfront Park, and Dojaebi-gol Haerang Observatory, so explore them together and experience the charm of Mukho Port.

※ Recommended route: Mukho Port → Nongol Damgil → Mukho Lighthouse → Lighthouse Cafe → Dojaebigol Sky Valley → Mukho Port Waterfront Park
