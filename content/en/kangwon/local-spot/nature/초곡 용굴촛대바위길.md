Located at the edge of Chogok Port in Geundeok-myeon, Samcheok City, this is a coastal attraction where the blue East Sea meets dramatic rock formations. For over 60 years, civilians were banned from entering as a military operation zone, but its stunning beauty was finally revealed in 2019. 
A place with stunning scenery and geological value, so remarkable it is called the 'Haegumgang of Samcheok'! Enjoy a special coastal walk while taking in the breathtaking scenery shaped by the waves and the refreshing sound of the surf.

※ Tourist site route: Course Entrance → Observatory → Photo Zone & Plaza → Suspension Bridge → Candle Rock → Turtle Rock & Pyramid Rock → Dragon Cave → Lion Rock
