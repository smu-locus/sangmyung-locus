Cheongoksan Yukbaekmaji in Mitan-myeon, Pyeongchang, is a place name given because it is a plain wide enough to sow six hundred bush clovers. Here, the Shasta daisies—nicknamed the “fried egg flowers”—are not flashy, but they bloom widely and abundantly across the entire meadow, making it beautiful. Photo zones are scattered throughout, including a charming castle-shaped sculpture and rainbow chairs with mountain ridges as a backdrop. A picturesque scene awaits you under the open sky—why not hike up the mountain trail and see it for yourself?

※ Recommended route: Byeolcheonji Village Hall → Cheongoksan Summit → Yukbaekmaji → Hanchi-dong
