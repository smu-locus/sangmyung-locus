Soyanggang Dam is the largest multipurpose dam in Korea, with a height of 123 meters, a levee length of 530 meters, and a storage capacity of 2.9 billion tons, so it does not open its gates during most rain. So whenever the gates open, crowds gather to witness the spectacular view. Of course, there’s plenty to do even on regular days, but the Dam Summit Trail, a round-trip walking path across the dam to the Octagonal Pavilion Observatory, is usually open, though access is restricted depending on weather conditions. Soyang Lake, the artificial lake created by the Soyanggang Dam, is so grand that it flows across Chuncheon, Yanggu, and Inje, and boasts beautiful scenery. You can take a cruise around the lake from the dock near the dam or visit Cheongpyeongsa Temple across the lake. In addition, between the dam and the pier, you can find attractions such as the Soyanggang Dam Water Culture Center, the Soyanggang Dam Completion Memorial Tower, and the small Soyanggang Maiden statue, while the road to the dam is lined with dakgalbi restaurants and cafes and filled with cherry blossom trees. 
Spend a meaningful time at Soyanggang Dam, a place that offers a different scenery with every season!

※ Cruise route: Soyanggang Dam Pier → Board the cruise → Cheongpyeongsa Temple (or Obongsan) boat trip
