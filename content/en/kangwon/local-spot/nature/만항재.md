Manhangjae is the highest mountain pass in Korea accessible by car, located on the border where Jeongseon, Taebaek, and Yeongwol in Gangwon Province meet. The pure white world spread across the high ridge and the layers of waves of the Baekdudaegan beneath your feet offer a sense of wonder just by standing still and gazing.
This place is called the 'Garden of the Heavens' because you can comfortably drive up to enjoy the stunning natural scenery of the Baekdudaegan mountain range and its beautiful rare wildflowers. From spring to autumn, a variety of wildflowers bloom abundantly, and in the early mornings, mist drifts in, creating a dreamy atmosphere. While the scenery is beautiful in every season, Manhangjae’s true charm lies in winter, as its high altitude and cold climate create a magical snow-covered landscape throughout the year.
The road up to Manhangjae has many S-curves, but since it’s a popular driving route, enjoy the beautiful scenery along the winding path!

※ Recommended route: Manhangjae → Hambaksan Prayer Altar → Hambaksan Summit
