Gugok Waterfall, located near Bonghwa Mountain, is named for its nine winding drops and is also known as Guguri Waterfall. White streams cascading down jagged rock formations from a height of 47 meters offer stunning scenery, and especially in winter, the massive ice wall provides a grand spectacle that draws many people to climb it. 
The path from the ticket booth to Gugok Waterfall is a relaxing walk through a blend of forest and valley. Along the trail, there are wooden plaques featuring the 'Nine Virtues of Gugokhon,' nine virtues starting with the letter 'kk,' such as dreams, talent, cunning, and grit, making it fun to find them one by one. Crossing the ridge known as Kkaldakgogae, you’ll reach the natural village of Munbae, which has nine restaurants where you can enjoy wild vegetable bibimbap and traditional liquors. 
Chuncheon’s Gugok Waterfall delights the eyes, palate, and heart—how about blowing away your everyday stress here?
