Hwaam Cave, located in the Hwaam Tourist Complex in Jeongseon County, Gangwon Province, is Korea’s first themed cave combining a gold mine and limestone. During the operation of the Cheonpo Mine, which extracted gold in the Japanese colonial era, a large limestone cave was discovered underground, leading to its development as an educational and experiential cave tourist site.
Of the total 1,803-meter viewing section, the 515-meter upper tunnel simulates the entire process from the discovery of the gold vein to mining, adding historical and educational significance. Inside the cave, you can observe a variety of formations, including large stalactites and stalagmites, stalactites, petrified wood, curved stones, and cave corals. In addition, there is a character-themed area featuring the cave mascots 'Geumkkaebi' and 'Eunkkaebi,' making it an engaging exploration experience for both children and adults.
Hwaam Cave, where nature’s mysterious landscapes blend with the history of the mining industry. Explore this unique cave world and create unforgettable memories in Jeongseon!
