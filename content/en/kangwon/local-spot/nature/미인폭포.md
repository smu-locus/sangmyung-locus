Miin Waterfall, located in the mountain valley south of Simpo-ri in Dogye-eup, between Tong-ri and Sin-ri in Taebaek City, is a waterfall where streams originating from the Ubo Mountain Valley of Simpo-ri crash against the cliffs; the smaller drops resemble a fountain, while the larger ones evoke the image of a snow-capped mountain. Famous for its rare emerald waters, this waterfall draws many visitors with its blend of a red gorge shaped by centuries of erosion and a mysterious light display. The 10-kilometer-long 'V'-shaped canyon shares the same formation process and geological characteristics as the Grand Canyon in the United States, earning it the nickname 'Korea's Grand Canyon,' and its overall color is red. This is because the sedimentary rocks were exposed to the air and oxidized under dry climatic conditions, rather than being deposited in river water.
Also, several stories are passed down about the name of Miin Waterfall. Near the waterfall is a tomb of a beauty; according to legend, she had left home to become a nun, but after her husband died early, she returned, and when he died again, she jumped off this waterfall. Another story says that after her husband died, she looked for a husband to marry but could not find one as good as her late husband, and despairing over her situation, she jumped to her death at this waterfall, which is how the name 'Beauty’s Waterfall' came to be. There is also an old story about predicting the weather at Beauty Falls; according to legend, if a warm wind blew from the falls before sunset or before sunrise, it would signal a bountiful harvest, while a cold wind would predict a poor one.  
Where nature’s stunning colors meet a poignant legend, is Beauty Falls. Don't miss this breathtaking exotic scenery unfolding before your eyes!
