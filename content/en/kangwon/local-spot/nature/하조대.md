Hajodae is a rocky coastline in Hyeonbuk-myeon, Yangyang County, Gangwon Province, covering 135,000 square meters, composed of striking rock formations and rocky islets rising into the deep blue East Sea, and its scenery is outstanding when blended with surrounding pine forests.  The name 'Hajodae' was given by Ha Ryun and Jo Jun, founding figures of the Joseon Dynasty, who lived in seclusion here at the end of Goryeo and sought to lead a revolution; it was named after their surnames. 
Hajodae Jeongja was first built during the reign of King Jeongjong of Joseon, and after being destroyed by fire in the Korean War, it was restored to its current form. It is famous for its beautiful scenery from the pavilion, and the towering old pine tree atop the dramatic cliffs was even featured in the national anthem broadcast, earning it the nickname 'Patriotic Pine.' If you come down from the pavilion and follow the deck path, you’ll find the pristine white Hajodae Lighthouse. Also known as the 'Gisamun Lighthouse,' this unmanned lighthouse was first built in May 1962 and can be seen from about 20 km away. The Hajodae Dulle-gil, where you can experience the majestic rocky cliffs and sea, is connected to an observation deck and is easy to walk, so even children and seniors can enjoy it. Also, Hajojdae Beach is located 1 km northwest of the island, where you can enjoy a variety of marine activities. 
A place where dramatic cliffs meet the blue East Sea! Create memories of a relaxing summer day at Hajodae.
