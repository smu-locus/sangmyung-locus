Located on the road from Osaek-ri in Yangyang County, Gangwon Province, where the Five-Color Mineral Spring is, toward Osaekryeong (Hangyeryeong), this is Seoraksan’s premier autumn foliage course, famous for its breathtaking scenery blending valleys and dramatic rock formations. ‘Osaek’ (Five Colors) is named after the five colors that the rock of Jujeon Valley emits and the tree said to bloom five-colored flowers in spring at the ancient Osaeksa Temple. Two stories are passed down about the place name 'Jujeongol.' One theory says the name comes from a steamed rice cake-shaped rock in front of Yongso Waterfall that looks like a stack of coins, while another says it originated from a story of bandits being caught while hiding in the valley and making counterfeit currency.
Located in the southern part of Seoraksan National Park, this area is famous for its five-colored mineral springs and hot springs, and it also features beautiful waterfalls such as Yongso Waterfall, the Twelve Waterfalls, and Goddess Waterfall. At the trailhead, you can taste the five-colored mineral spring, which has a fizzy, iron-rich flavor, and there is also a nearby hot spring, so after a leisurely walk along the autumn leaves and valley, relax and relieve your travel fatigue in a warm bath.

※ Recommended route: Osaek Mineral Spring Visitor Center → Osaek Mineral Spring → Seonnyeo Bath → Geumgangmun Gate → Yongso Waterfall Visitor Center
