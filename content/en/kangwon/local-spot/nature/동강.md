The Donggang is a major river where the Odaecheon, which flows from Odaesan, and the Joyang River in northern Jeongseon merge and flow into Yeongwol. This river winds and meanders along the mountainside, forming a serpentine shape, and features sheer cliffs throughout its course. Because the Donggang River is not easily accessible, its pristine scenery and rich ecosystem have been well preserved, making the many caves and fish reefs here feel mysterious. 
This place offers ideal conditions for a variety of water sports and is perfect for children to play in the water. In addition, the Yeongwol Donggang Festival is held every July and August, offering various hands-on experiences such as catching trout with bare hands, and from December to January, a winter festival provides a wide range of activities including lure fishing, paragliding, and sledding. In addition, the area around Donggang is home to historical sites such as Cheongnyeongpo, where King Danjong was exiled, Jagur, the Wanggeomseong and Jeongyang fortresses known from the Three Kingdoms period, and the thousand-year-old Beopheungsa Temple, making it a great place to enjoy both nature and culture. Set off to the Donggang River, where you can experience beautiful nature, exciting activities, and rich history.
