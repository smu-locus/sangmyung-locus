Although Nami Island belongs to Chuncheon, Gangwon Province, you have to go to the Nami Island pier in Gapyeong, Gyeonggi Province to get there. With the concept of a “land of fairy tales and an island of songs,” this place has consistently hosted a variety of cultural events, concerts, and exhibitions, providing children with dreams and hope, lovers with love and memories, and cultural figures with a foundation and space for creativity as a natural ecological cultural hub.The main facilities include cultural amenities such as a song museum, picture book playground, MICE center, Together the Park, and a UNICEF lounge, as well as recreational facilities like a bicycle park, sharing train, and story tour bus. There is also Hotel Jeonggwanru, which offers lodging with a diverse menu, themed rooms, and an annex with a private riverside space. 
From concerts to a variety of cultural events and exhibitions, create exciting memories at Nami Island where fun is always guaranteed!
