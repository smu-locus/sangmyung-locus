Located in Yeoryang-myeon, Jeongseon, this place is where Songcheon, which originates in Doam-myeon, Pyeongchang County and flows down Gujeolli, and Goljicheon, which originates in Hajang-myeon, Samcheok and flows toward Imgae, meet; it is called 'Auraji' because the two streams blend here. According to feng shui, during the summer monsoon, if the water on the Yangsu side, Songcheon, is abundant, a great flood will occur, while if the Yin water, Goljicheon, is abundant, the monsoon will end; above all, it is especially famous for the melody of Jeongseon Arirang, a designated intangible cultural asset of Gangwon Province. 
Ride a ferry from Auraji, the birthplace of the 'Love Song' of Jeongseon Arirang, where unspoken feelings have become song, and feel its tender atmosphere!

※ Tourist course: Bachelor Statue → Crescent Bridge → Maiden Statue → Suspension Bridge → Stone Bridge (or stepping stone bridge)
