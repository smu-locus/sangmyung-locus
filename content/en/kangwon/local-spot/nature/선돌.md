Seondol, located in Bangjeol-ri, Yeongwol County, Gangwon State, is a famous spot where a 70-meter-high standing stone rises atop a layered cliff where the Seogang River winds dramatically. Seondol, also known as Sinsunam, offers a view where the blue river and layered cliffs blend together, making you feel as if you are looking at a painting. There is also a legend that Danjong stopped here for a brief rest at a spot where a standing stone could be seen on his way to Cheongnyeongpo (a scenic site in Yeongwol), and because the stone stood so majestically it resembled an immortal, it came to be called 'Seondol." 
A breathtaking view as if a slice of Mount Kumgang had been brought here! Meet it in person at Seondol in Yeongwol.
