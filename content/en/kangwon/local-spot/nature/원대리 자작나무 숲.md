The birch forest in Wondae-ri, near Inje-eup, is a natural ecotourism site where you can experience the ecological, aesthetic, and educational values unique to birch trees, and is also known as the “whispering birch forest.” From 1974 to 1995, 690,000 birch trees were planted over 138 hectares to create this area, which features seven hiking trails, a forest classroom, an observatory, an ecological pond, an Indian-style house, a wooden bridge, and wooden stairs, making it a perfect place for forest bathing and relaxation. In winter, the snow-covered forests and pure white birch trees make it a popular photography destination, and families with children can enjoy various nature-themed activities such as forest classrooms and Indian houses at the forest experience center. Starting at the birch forest information center, a forest trail unfolds with dense stands of birch trees over 20 years old, and the gleaming white trunks create an exotic atmosphere as if snow had fallen. 
When you want to heal or be alone, experience the white beauty embroidered by dense birch trees!
