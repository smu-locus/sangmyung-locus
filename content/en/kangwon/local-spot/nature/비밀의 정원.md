The Secret Garden, which you can reach by passing through Gapdun-ri Road, is known as a popular spot for landscape photography. If you visit early in the morning, you can see the mystical frost and fog, making it a place where every scene feels beautiful. This place is called the Secret Garden because it is a military operation zone and not easily accessible to the public; photography was previously completely banned, but now even taking pictures from the roadside is allowed. 
Meet a special moment nature offers amid the mystical scenery created by early morning fog and frost!
