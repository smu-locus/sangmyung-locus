Hwajinpo Lake was named for the blooming rose of Sharon along its shore, and it is the largest natural lake on the East Coast with a circumference of 16 km. Although this lagoon was formed in Geojin-eup, Goseong County, it is adjacent to the sea, allowing freshwater and seawater to flow without separation, and the surrounding dense forests of Korean red pines offer Goseong’s breathtaking natural beauty. It has also been home to many famous villas throughout history; today, the Hwajinpo Memorial Hall & Villa of President Syngman Rhee and the Villa of Lee Ki-bung, as well as the Villa of Kim Il-sung, are operated as a historical security exhibition center.
Discover the true beauty of Goseong at Hwajinpo Lake, where nature and history coexist!
