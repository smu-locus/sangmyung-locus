Designated by the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs as a farm specialized in animal welfare and forest conservation, it lets sheep graze freely on wide pastures. After completing its first livestock facility in 1989 and introducing sheep farming in 1991, this place has grown into a farm with one of the best sheep-raising systems in the country. There are two main things to enjoy at Daegwallyeong Sheep Ranch: walking the trails and feeding the sheep. Especially on the trails, you can walk among wild plants and see sheep grazing in the meadows. 
Take a leisurely walk with the sheep and experience the beautiful scenery of the Baekdudaegan mountain range at the end of your journey!
