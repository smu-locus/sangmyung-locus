Bugwi-ri Cherry Blossom Road stretches along the path leading into Bugwi-ri, a mountain village in Buksan-myeon, located between Chuncheon, Hwacheon, and Yanggu. It's a place you have to drive about 40 to 50 minutes from downtown Chuncheon to reach. Because it is one of the coldest regions inland, the cherry blossoms bloom late, and even as the city’s trees turn green, the cherry trees here remain vibrant, as if waiting for late visitors. Looking at the cherry blossoms, like delicate snowflakes, scattered across the blue spring sky, you can feel a distinctly different sense of warmth and charm here than in the city. 
For those who missed the cherry blossom viewing due to a busy schedule, these are the last remaining cherry blossom spots like a gift box! Geonbongryeong Seunghodae, a Milky Way attraction, is just a 15–20 minute drive away, so be sure to visit there as well!
