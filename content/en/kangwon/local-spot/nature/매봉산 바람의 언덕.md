Maebongsan Wind Hill is a plateau area at 1,200–1,300 meters above sea level, where massive windmills blend with open highland cabbage fields to create a scene straight out of a fairy tale. The biggest appeal is that a cool breeze blows even in midsummer, offering a refreshing feeling, and you can enjoy an open, unobstructed sense of space while gazing at the surrounding mountains and fields—something hard to experience in the city. It is also famous as a spot to watch the sunrise and sea of clouds, and after sunset, the starlight stretching beyond the vast cabbage fields creates a magical view. 
When you want to escape the suffocating routine and face breathtaking scenery, head to Maebongsan Wind Hill!
