The Vietnam Deployment Veterans Meeting Place is the nation’s first and only comprehensive memorial space related to the Vietnam deployment, a security and historical educational tourism site created to honor the history of the deployed soldiers and boost the pride of those who served. The former Pauwol Training Center, which was used for training deployed soldiers, has been refurbished and reborn as the current memorial space. 
Centered around a memorial hall reflecting on the history and significance of the deployment, this site includes a barracks-style lodging, an outdoor picnic area, and a restored former kitchen. There is also a Vietnamese reenactment village (Gucci Tunnel, traditional houses) and a combat equipment exhibition area (tanks, self-propelled artillery, armored vehicles, etc.), allowing visitors to vividly experience the atmosphere of the time. Under the theme of 'Understanding and Harmony,' this space serves as a multifaceted historical experience center that fosters exchange with Vietnam while providing education on security history and promoting the region. 
Take time to reflect on the history and legacy of the Vietnam deployment and reflect on the meaning of history and the value of peace!
