The Gangwon Museum of History was established and has been operated in December 2004 by the Daejin Education Foundation, applying traditional architectural styles from both the East and the West. It exhibits over 20,000 artifacts from around the world, including natural history items, ceramics, metalwork, folk art, religious artifacts, woodworking, and stonecraft. 
We also collect, preserve, research, exchange, and exhibit artifacts here to help people better understand the environment in which humanity lives. In other words, the museum has established itself as a lifelong learning institution by planning exhibitions that allow people to experience the lifestyle of each era, continuously updating its displays, and engaging with the local community. 
At the Gangwon Museum of Natural History, where you can see natural history artifacts from around the world, spend a day of learning and enjoyment!
