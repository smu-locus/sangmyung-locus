Located at an elevation of 70 meters above sea level in Myeongho-ri, Hyeonnae-myeon, Goseong County, Gangwon State, the northernmost point of the East Coast, this site is the heart of the 'Goseong DMZ.' It was built in 1984 to reflect on the pain of division and the hope for reunification, with the reality of the division unfolding beneath our feet. From here, you can see most of Haegeumgang with a distance of about 16 km at the closest and 25 km at the farthest, including the surrounding islands, Manmulsang (Saja Rock), Hyeonjongam, Sagongam, and Buddha Rock. You can also visit facilities such as the Unification Security Park, the Korean War Experience Exhibition Hall, the Unification Hall, and the DMZ Museum, making for a meaningful time to reflect on the history of division and the meaning of peace.
