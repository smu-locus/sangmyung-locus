Located on Gyeongpo-ro in Gangneung, Gangwon Province, this is a pavilion on the northern hill of Gyeongpo Lake and is one of the Eight Views of Gwandong, showcasing Goryeo-era culture. It was originally built in the 13th year of King Chungsuk of Goryeo and has undergone one reconstruction and several repairs to take its current form. Inside Gyeongpodae, you can find numerous writings by notable figures and poets, including 'Eoje-si,' composed by King Sukjong himself, “Gyeongpodae-bu,” said to have been written by the scholar Yulgok Yi I at age ten, and the beam-raising inscription by Jo Hamang. Also nearby is Gyeongpo Lake, where old pine forests and cherry trees blend, and the Haeansa-ju, which separates Gyeongpo Lake from the East Sea, forms Gyeongpo Beach. Above all, since this place is famous for moon-gazing, how about spending some time strolling around Gyeongpodae at dusk when the moon rises?
