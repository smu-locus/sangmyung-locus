Cheorwon Historical and Cultural Park is a miniature replica of the economically prosperous Cheorwon town area of the 1930s, with buildings from the old town restored exactly as they were based on photographs from that time. As you walk along the central street, you’ll find cultural shops selling souvenirs and local specialties, as well as retro-style tea houses, where you can soak in the nostalgic atmosphere. 
This place is divided into a modern cultural street and a history exhibition and experience center, where you can enjoy both sightseeing and hands-on activities. There is a general information center at the entrance where you can get a guidebook, which is very helpful for understanding the exhibits. Especially on weekends, an outdoor performance called 'Modern Times' takes place against the backdrop of the street square, offering a rich variety of sights to enjoy. 
Stroll through the streets of Cheorwon in the 1930s, encounter traces of modern history, and fully enjoy a retro journey back in time!
