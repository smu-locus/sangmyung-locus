The Hanseo Namgung Eok Memorial Hall honors Mr. Namgung Eok, who practiced love for his country with a passion unlike anyone else during the Japanese colonial period. In addition to the memorial hall, there is the former chapel, Mugunghwa Park, and the Hanseo Cemetery. This place introduces materials and photographs about Namgung Eok’s biography and achievements, and next to the exhibition hall, a chapel that was forcibly closed during the Japanese colonial period has been restored. If you haven’t met the figures you’ve likely seen and learned about in history textbooks during their lifetimes but want to learn more about them, this is a great place to visit. Also, there are plenty of spaces to relax, making it a great place to take a break. 
Start a day of getting closer to the figures in your textbooks at the Hanseo Namgung Ok Memorial Hall!
