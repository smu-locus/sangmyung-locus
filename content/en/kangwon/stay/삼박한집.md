Sambakhan House is a seaside retreat in Goseong, Gangwon Province, newly built by a butler with 20 years of experience in the hospitality industry. Authentic Japanese hinoki cypress baths are available in all rooms for guests born in 1980. It has a three-sided bed guard and 18 types of baby products. It received the Gangwon Architecture Culture Award Special Prize, was designed by a Seoul Public Architecture firm, and was selected for the Ministry of Culture and Tourism’s Safe Stay program.
