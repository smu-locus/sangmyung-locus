Located in the tourist hotspot of Donghae, the New Donghae Tourist Hotel offers a comfortable and relaxing experience along with attentive service. Each room is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, including communication systems, and offers dining and beverage amenities such as a premium Italian restaurant and café, as well as facilities like a coin laundry and fitness center. In addition, depending on the purpose of the meeting or event, guests can enjoy programs planned by a specialized banquet team that matches the hotel’s reputation, offering a variety of settings such as business rooms, banquet halls, and meeting rooms, as well as catering for corporate events or family gatherings, and for events ranging from small boutique weddings to large-scale weddings. Designed to combine exotic charm with Korean sentiment, it offers a modern and sophisticated atmosphere while ensuring comfort, welcoming guests with warm and attentive top-quality service.
