Odaesan Bogung Guesthouse is a condo-style homestay in Jinbu-myeon, Pyeongchang-gun, converted from an old building, offering a comfortable and cozy place to relax. There are four rooms, featuring a clean, single-story interior with a cozy yellow earth room and a cypress wood room, allowing guests to stay cool in summer and warm in winter. There is parking available for guests in front of the guesthouse, making it convenient, and it offers views of beautiful natural scenery.
