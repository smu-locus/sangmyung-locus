Amaranth in Sokcho, Gangwon Province, is a business hotel where each room’s terrace offers a panoramic view of Ulsan Rock in Seoraksan. Compared to the city, it has a tranquil atmosphere that is very calming. Since remodeling the rooms in 2020 with minimalist simplicity, the hotel has consistently maintained quality and received hygiene certification from Sokcho City. Sokcho IC, Seoraksan, Cheoksan Hot Springs, Yeongrangho, and the sea are all within a 5–15 minute drive, making transportation convenient.
