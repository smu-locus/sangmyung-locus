Sonovel&Sono Calm Vivaldi Park is a comprehensive resort where the nature of all four seasons, leisure, and relaxation come together. Set within the beautiful natural environment of Hongcheon, Gangwon Province, this all-in-one resort offers a variety of leisure activities such as lodging, skiing, and a water park in one place, making it a space that satisfies a wide range of travelers, from families and couples to groups. Based on seasonal specialized content, comfortable rooms, and a well-organized operational infrastructure, it provides high-quality services that prioritize guests’ safety and convenience, offering refined relaxation and enjoyment so that the stay itself becomes a special experience.
