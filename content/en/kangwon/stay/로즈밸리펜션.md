Rose Valley is located along National Route 7, boasting some of South Korea’s best drive-by beach scenery. The pension is situated on a spacious 1,500-pyeong (approximately 4,950 square meters) site, with a stream flowing in front, a large lawn, and various amenities. In addition, it is close to Imwon Port, famous for sashimi, and the beach, allowing you to enjoy the sea, mountains, and valleys all at once. You can also stay in a private pension, making it perfect for group, couple, or family accommodations.
Nearby linked tourist attractions include Jangho Beach, a marine rail bike, transparent canoeing, snorkeling, Chuam Sunrise, Daegum Cave, Hwanseon Cave, Haesindang Park, Deokpung Valley trekking, Geombongsan Forest Recreation Area, and Deokgu Hot Springs, offering a variety of healing and leisure experiences.
