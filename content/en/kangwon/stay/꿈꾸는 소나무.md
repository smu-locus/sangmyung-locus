Hoengseong Pension Dreaming Pine is a healing and relaxation-style pension located in front of Dunnae Welli Hilli Park in Hoengseong County, Gangwon Province. The pension faces the Welli Hilli Park slopes and is surrounded by dense pine forests, offering a refreshing and clear natural environment. In summer, an outdoor pool is available for water activities, and the decked walking paths along with seasonal flower gardens make it a great place for a leisurely stroll. The rooms vary, including private Merigold villas, duplexes, and spa-equipped units, all featuring private terraces and individual barbecue areas. Therefore, personal rest is possible. With a first aid kit, it provides a safe environment for families staying with children or elderly guests. They also provide free locally grown organic vegetables, charcoal barbecue grill rentals, and KTX Dunnae Station pickup service. It is also just a 3-minute drive from Welli Hilli Park Ski Resort, Water Planet, and Welli Hilli Country Club, making it ideal for leisure and sports activities.
