Healience Seon Village in Hongcheon County, Gangwon Province, is a well-aging healing resort created in collaboration with Dr. Lee Si-hyung, a psychiatrist and neuroscientist. Located halfway up the hill of Jongja Mountain, it offers a chance to relax in complete nature. Located halfway up the hill of Jongja Mountain, it offers a chance to experience peaceful relaxation in untouched nature. There are a total of 92 rooms, including the Forest Wing, Garden Wing, Ochre Room, and the House of Wind and Earth, which embraces healthy natural energy, and some rooms are pet-friendly.
Food and beverage facilities such as restaurants, cafes, and wine bars, as well as healing spaces like spas, jjimjilbangs, carbonated water saunas, therapy centers, and loess trails, are available. Visitors can relax in various ways according to their preferences, whether by gazing at the campfire in the fire zone, calming their minds in the meditation room, or enjoying music and books in the LP music room and library. It also features cultural spaces such as a gallery, workshop, and seminar room, as well as wellness, a Healy Pet area, a kids’ space, and convenience facilities. In addition, healing experience programs such as forest therapy, small-objects therapy, and singing bowl meditation are offered to promote physical and mental relaxation.
