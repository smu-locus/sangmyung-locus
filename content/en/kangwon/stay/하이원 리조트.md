High1 Resort is a large-scale integrated leisure complex in Sabuk-eup, Jeongseon-gun, Gangwon Province, featuring a ski resort, golf course, casino, and accommodations. Based on a wide range of leisure, cultural, and relaxation content available year-round, it provides customers with special experiences and refined services. In winter, they operate a ski resort featuring 18 slopes of varying difficulty levels, ensuring that everyone from beginners to experts can enjoy the slopes safely. In summer, Hi-One Water World operates with pure valley water from the Baekdudaegan mountain range. In spring and fall, the resort adds to its year-round appeal with a variety of nature-friendly activities such as golf and trekking, as well as hotel condo facilities and wellness programs for the whole family.
