Osaek Green Yard Hotel is a place to enjoy a healthy retreat, offering more than just accommodation with its hot springs and immune-boosting programs. Situated in the forest at 647 meters above sea level in Seoraksan, the hotel focuses on restoring guests’ health through fresh air, natural hot springs sourced from Seoraksan, and a variety of wellness programs. The core of the Osaek Green Yard Hotel lies in its two types of hot springs. These are the high-temperature alkaline hot springs gushing from 650 meters above sea level at Osaekryeong (Hangyeryeong) in Seoraksan, and the 27°C carbonated spring water, which was self-developed and pumped up to 470 meters underground from the accommodation. After soaking in the hot spring water, you can enhance your immunity while enjoying a relaxing time with a rock wave bath—a detox thermal therapy where you lie on rocks at around 50°C to eliminate toxins. There are four room categories, from a standard double room to a five-colored suite for six people.
