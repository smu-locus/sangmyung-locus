Hotel Inter Burgo Wonju in Wonju, Gangwon Province, faces Mount Chiak from the entire rear of the building, each boasting picturesque scenery unique to every season. 'Inter burgo' is a Spanish word meaning a peaceful little village. This property offers approximately 145 rooms designed to provide comfort and relaxation. It is also the only convention hotel in Wonju offering a breakfast buffet made with fresh ingredients daily, a golf course in the city, and a classy wedding hall with banquet facilities for seminars, workshops, and international events, along with tailored professional consulting to support successful business.
