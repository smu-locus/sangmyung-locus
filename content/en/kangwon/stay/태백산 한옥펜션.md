Taebaeksan Hanok Pension is a traditional house built from about 200-year-old red pine trees, located in a pristine plateau area surrounded by Taebaeksan and Hanbaeksan mountains, where the scenery remains clear and beautiful throughout all four seasons. In summer, you can gather in the backyard to eat watermelon or enjoy a barbecue, and in winter, you can create small memories by roasting sweet potatoes over a fire in the traditional stove. Traditional experiences such as traditional weddings, yutnori (a Korean board game), and hoop rolling are also available. Nearby attractions include the Cheoram Mining Village and Guwau Village, where a sunflower festival is held.
