Byuliden Saem Pension is a space where a variety of travelers—families, friends, and couples—can relax and enjoy their leisure time, thanks to its comfortable rooms and peaceful surroundings. The pension provides in-room cooking facilities and parking, along with various amenities such as a barbecue area, vegetable garden, bicycle rentals, and a mini soccer field. A highlight is the opportunity to enjoy fresh vegetables such as lettuce, cucumbers, and perilla leaves grown in the community garden. It is also ideal for planning a trip that links the natural scenery of the Hantan River area with nearby tourist attractions, and it offers accommodation for visitors interested in outdoor leisure activities such as rafting.
