Outdoor Family is a Western-style studio pension built with Canadian timber, located along the Naerincheon River 6 km from Inje-eup along National Route 31. It also offers a spacious parking lot and guest rooms for individual outdoor enthusiasts. You can cook and dine in the room, and the air conditioning ensures a comfortable stay. The view of Naerincheon from the outdoor deck is spectacular, and the wood-paneled interior naturally regulates humidity, adding to the comfort. The spacious central staircase, which can seat over 40 people, is also famous as a popular spot for group photos.
